Termvoya Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Records and settings

Termvoya stores your purchase options, contracts, payments, notes, selected contacts, and selected photos in local app storage. You can use it without creating an account. The app does not send these records to a developer-operated server or include advertising or tracking.

Dictation and Apple Intelligence

When you request dictation, Termvoya uses the microphone with your permission to transcribe the draft on your device. On compatible devices, Apple Intelligence can organize draft text on your device. Review or edit the results before saving. Termvoya does not save recordings as part of your purchase records.

Photos, contacts and reminders

Termvoya uses items you select in the system photo or contacts picker. It can use the camera after you grant access. Reminders are scheduled locally when you enable them. Manage permissions in iOS Settings.

Retention, deletion and export

Records stay in your local store until you delete them or remove the app. You can keep or discard a draft and decide when to export your records. Copies you save or share are managed at their destination. iOS backup settings may govern copies of local app data.

Support

If you contact support, we use the information you send to respond to your request. You can contact mohsalahnassar@gmail.com about privacy or request deletion of support correspondence.
