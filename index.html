Get in touch

Email mohsalahnassar@gmail.com for help. Include the app version, iOS version, and what you were doing when the issue occurred. Keep your account numbers, contracts, and other sensitive details private.

Adding and tracking a purchase

Use Add to describe a purchase or enter it by hand. Save it as an option to compare, or confirm the installment terms to track it. The app records payments you've made.

Apple Intelligence availability

Apple Intelligence can organize notes on compatible devices when enabled. Use manual entry whenever you prefer or if the device does not support Apple Intelligence.

Exporting records

Open Settings, then Export records. Choose whether to include photos and use the system file picker to save your copy.
